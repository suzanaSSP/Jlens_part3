- delta = similarity - baseline
    A lot of the categories have the same common tokens. 
    The baseline it made up of the things in common in the distributions of Law, Politics & Government, Science, Physics & Math, and Teachnology distributions
    The category's similarity score minus this baseline removes the common tokens similarity to refine the specific category's similarity

- Window distribution
    When calculate the JS divergence for each position, the results come very spiky. 
    To smooth things out we use a window, where we get the distrbution mean for 10 positions and compute the distribution differnce from the category's GT from there.
    Then the next window moves forward one, so let's say it start at 1-10, next window is 2-11

- Ground Truths
    Let's say you run 30 religious questions through J-space. Average out the probability distrbution of the tokens in the Jspace.
    Create a distribution out of it, you now have a Ground Truth Religious Distribution. This means that this is an "ideal" representation of religion in the Jspace. 
    This can be done to all the categories. If you take the KL Divergence of a distribution X and this religious GT, that difference is how similar distribution X is to the religious GT.
