Benchmark J-Space Category Streams

For each benchmark question, how similar the model's internal j-space output is to each of 7 tracked category ground truths (plus "Other," the summed baseline categories), across normalized token position through thought and answer. Similarity is 1 − Jensen-Shannon divergence to that category's ground-truth distribution. Pick a question below, or start with the aggregate across all questions and the ranked religion-signal overview.

Raw similarity curves move almost in lockstep across categories: the same per-position distribution is compared against every ground truth, and generic/high-frequency tokens overlap all of them, so a confident or diffuse position shifts every category's similarity together. Differential subtracts the mean of the 3 baseline categories (Law/Politics, Technology, Science — chosen for having no plausible category-specific content) at each position, isolating signal specific to that category from this shared drift.
